Sun Dial

The sun circles the places you care about. Your home sits at the top, so the sun is overhead when it's true noon there. Lit places are having their day; shaded ones are in their night.

Now

    The places shown at first are examples. The sun moves at its real position: places sit by longitude, not by time zone. For simplicity, every place gets 12 hours of light, as if it were always the equinox. "Sun time" is local solar time, so noon means the sun is at its highest. The land and sea on the disc are decoration, not a map.
